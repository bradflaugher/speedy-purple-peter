# SPEEDY PURPLE PETER title screen (20x18 tiles).
#
# @titlekey C=T_NAME ...   legend for the scene ('.' is T_SKY)
# @titlescene y=N          the scene in world metatiles, 10 per row. y=-1: the first metatile
#                          row covers tile rows -1..0 (clipped), so metatile row k sits on tile
#                          rows 2k-1 and 2k and the ground ends on tile row 10.
# @titleart col=C row=R pal=P   logo pixels placed at tile (C, R) over the scene. Its tiles are
#                          deduplicated into title_tiles (<= 48, loaded at BG tile 208); empty
#                          8x8 tiles are left to the scene. Spaces in rows are ignored.
#
# Rows 11-17 stay plain sky: the engine prints PRESS START, the seed and the best score there.
# The scene may only use world tiles numbered below 128 (the generator places them first).
# Logo letters: colour 1 face, colour 2 lower shade, colour 3 outline. The logo and its streaks
# are all purple on CGB (PAL_SKY: violet / deep purple / pale lilac; PAL_DECOR: indigo / lilac /
# pale glow): never PAL_HUD or PAL_GOLD, no yellow.
#
# The engine draws Peter sprinting (SPR_PB_DASH0/1) at screen x 16, y 40-71 (tile columns 2-3,
# rows 5-8) on the ground; the streak at (0, 5) is his trail (its column 0 flickers with his stride).

@titlekey a=T_STARS_A b=T_STARS_B P=T_PLANET_TL Q=T_PLANET_TR p=T_PLANET_BL q=T_PLANET_BR
@titlekey G=T_GROUND_TOP h=T_HILL_TOP L=T_HILL_L F=T_HILL_CRATER R=T_HILL_R
@titlekey 1=T_BUSH_L 2=T_BUSH_M 3=T_BUSH_R
